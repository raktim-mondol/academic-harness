## 0.43.0

Phases 3–6 of [ScientistTwo → Academic Harness](SCIENTISTTWO-ROADMAP.md): the advisory review score with evaluator provenance, the staged experiment contract, the ablation ledger, and limitation, trace, selection, and grounded-novelty records on the hypothesis tournament.

- **New `score` action on `deep_review`.** One bounded model call reads a stored review round and returns advisory `soundness`, `originality`, `clarity`, and `significance` readings on a declared numeric scale, each citing one to four stored comment ids. A dimension with no numeric reading or no valid comment id is `not_checked`, never guessed. The overall reading is the model's score or the mean of the scored dimensions (source recorded), with confidence and limitations. The scale and rubric source (`caller`, `setting`, or `default`) are recorded; no acceptance decision, venue outcome, or gate is produced.
- **No model route is not a pass.** A session without the host LLM service, a failed call, or a non-JSON reply still completes and stores `score.json` with `state: not_checked` and the reason. `show section=score` and `status` read it back; `score.json` (`schemaVersion` 1) and `meta.score` are new artifacts on the existing v2 store, with the same v1 materialization as `validity`.
- **Evaluator provenance per round.** Every review round records `evaluator_kind: held_out | in_distribution`. The default is `held_out`; pass `rubric_round` on `review` when a prior round's rubric or report fed the revision, and the next round is recorded `in_distribution` with the referenced round. Old rounds and v1 stores are read as `held_out` with `evaluator_kind_source: inferred`. The score records the selected round's kind, and `show rounds`, `compare`, and `status` expose it.
- **Settings.** `DEEP_REVIEW_DEFAULT_RUBRIC` sets the score rubric and its declared scale; `DEEP_REVIEW_MAX_ROUNDS` refuses a review once that many rounds are stored; `DEEP_REVIEW_COST_CEILING_USD` checks the engine's median estimate before a review and refuses if it exceeds the ceiling. All three are read per call, default to no restriction, and are editable in Settings → Academic Harness API keys.
- **Phase 0 score correlation.** `scripts/deep-review-calibration.mjs --report` now reads stored `score.json`, joins the overall score to accepted/rejected decisions, and reports point-biserial `r`, AUC, mean scores by bucket, evaluator-kind counts, and a separate held-out subset. It prints `not_checked` below the existing sample floor instead of a number the data cannot carry. `scripts/calibration-metrics.mjs` holds the dependency-free metrics and has an offline test.
- **Wording.** `prompt.js`, `docs/PAPERS.md`, and `docs/WRITING.md` state the advisory posture explicitly: the score is an automated reading, not an acceptance decision, and nothing keys a gate off it.
- Tests: `scripts/test-deep-review-tool.mjs` covers the score, citation filtering, evaluator provenance, round limit, cost ceiling, scale source, and store/show/status surfaces; `scripts/test-deep-review-score-correlation.mjs` tests the metrics; `scripts/test-deep-review-calibration-report.mjs` exercises the report end-to-end on synthetic scored stores.
- **Phase 4: staged experimentation contract on `experiment_log`.** `start` now accepts a benchmark spec (`datasets`, `splits`, `baseline_arm`, `task_rules`) and an optional protocol (`eval_script` + `metric_params`); `log` accepts `role: baseline|variant`, `baseline_ref: reproduced|reported`, `stage: subset|slice|full`, and a sampling rule. The baseline captures `protocol_digest` (SHA-256 of the eval-script hash plus canonical metric parameters); every variant must name a matching baseline, and a changed script or parameter set is stored as `spec_drift` with expected/actual digests and changed components. `verdict` records an advisory human `good|engineer|bad` with reason, `engineering_round`, and budget; a budget-exhausted engineer is force-`bad`. `best dominance: true` returns the multi-metric Pareto front (incomplete runs are not ranked). `verdicts.jsonl`, stage/protocol fields, and drift are surfaced by `compare`, `show`, `status`, and the exports. `spec_drift` is now a sixth `provenance_audit` unsupported state, indexed from drifted experiment runs. Tests: `scripts/test-experiment-log.mjs` and `scripts/test-provenance-audit.mjs`.
- **Phase 5: `ablation` tool.** New `ablation.js` + `ablation-store.js` under `.dsh/ablation/<slug>/` with `start`, `plan`, `record`, `report`, `verdict`, `prune`, `show`, `status`, and `export`. `start` binds a method spec and champion idea id; `plan` freezes component × arm × dataset × metric arms with expected changes, datasets, metric directions, replicates, and a reference arm; `record` links a cell to an existing `experiment_log` run (metrics from that run) or an `analysis_run` with hashed outputs (the reading is supplied), so it never executes; `report` computes paired removal deltas against the reference, lists ties and missing cells, and gives an advisory attributed/unattributed/partial/not_checked reading; `verdict` records a human `good|refine|unattributed`; `prune` is append-only. It never claims a scientific result. Tests: `scripts/test-ablation-store.mjs` and `scripts/test-ablation-tool.mjs`; docs: ANALYSIS, TOOLS, and the `ablation-attribution` skill.
- **Phase 6: limitation records, traced evolution, selection, and grounded novelty on the tournament.** `coscientist` gains `limitation` (`add`/`verify`/`status`), `trace`, and `select`; `add` accepts `addresses_limitation`. Limitation records follow the paper's published artifact — a flat numbered list carrying the methodological flaw, a **verbatim source quote span** verified against a hypothesis or workspace text file, why it limits the method, and **at least two remedy families**. Sufficiency is binary `sufficient`/`insufficient`, the loop is round-capped (default 16, `CO_SCIENTIST_LIMITATION_ROUNDS`), and a spent cap is reported `exhausted` rather than accepted. Refinement is set-append only: nothing is rewritten or deleted. Coverage by active ideas is exposed by `status`, `graph` (limitation nodes and `addresses` edges), and both exports. `trace` reads `experiment_log` runs — failed and pruned first — and an `evolution` dispatch from `next` carries the failed/pruned traces and their reasons as constraints. `select` freezes an active champion with its compared survivors, rationale, and a SHA-256 digest over the tournament readings, review scores, citation/novelty checks, and matching experiment traces; it is history, not an acceptance gate. `coscientist_verify novelty` retrieves a small reference set (default 2, the paper's only published number; max 10), stores it, computes a fully documented lexical prior-art reading (`10 × (1 − max token coverage)` — ours, because the paper gives no formula, range, or threshold), and pins it to verbatim quote spans; a failed retrieval is stored `not_checked` with the reason and no score. Tests: `scripts/test-coscientist-store.mjs`, `test-coscientist-tool.mjs`, `test-coscientist-verify.mjs`, and `test-nonsearch-tools.mjs`; docs: RESEARCH, TOOLS, CONFIGURATION, ARCHITECTURE, this changelog, the roadmap, `prompt.js`, and the `co-scientist-tournament` skill (loop, limitation/selection section, and briefs §13).

## 0.42.0

Phase 2 of [ScientistTwo → Academic Harness](SCIENTISTTWO-ROADMAP.md): the evaluation-validity criteria, landed wrapper-side as one new action. Nothing in `vendor/` moved, so `VENDOR_COMMIT` stays unpinned-dragged.

- **New `validity` action on `deep_review`.** Four advisory evaluation-validity checks — `attribution`, `evaluator_drift`, `benchmark_coverage`, `unmeasured_trade_off` — run over one stored review round (`round` takes a round number or `run_id`, latest by default; `review.json` when that round is the latest and still stored, otherwise `rounds/<run_id>.json`) plus a deterministic inventory of the manuscript itself: its text (from `.tex`/`.md`/`.txt`, or the stored `paper.md` for a PDF when its recorded source hash matches the current file, else a fresh conversion), its results grids parsed from LaTeX `table`/`tabular`/`array`/`longtable` bodies and Markdown pipe rows, and the numeric half from `numbers_audit`, whose own row counter is blind to multi-column grids and so is not reused for counting them. Coverage axes and arm labels are aggregated across every parsed grid, so a second table can widen a thin first one. The four criteria are the paper's own AutoSOTA failure cases; the vocabularies and the cut-offs are the plugin's, because the paper states neither.
- **Three states, and none of them is a score.** `clear`, `flagged`, or `not_checked`, each carrying a reason string, line-level evidence, and tallies. A `flagged` state names an absence in the text, not a flaw in the work; `not_checked` is never a pass, and a manuscript that cannot be read leaves all four `not_checked` with the read error as the reason instead of a silent clean sheet. Counting is keyword and grid based: LaTeX prose is matched through a mask so comments, math, and commands cannot produce a hit, while the drift check reads comment-masked raw lines — a `\texttt` parameter change survives, a commented-out one does not. Markdown HTML comments and fenced code blocks are masked for every check. Inventory caps (400k characters, 40 grids, 60 rows per grid, 10 evidence items per check) are disclosed in the artifact.
- **One bounded critic call, on by default.** With `critic: true` the pass makes at most one model call — temperature 0, 8,000 output tokens (live reasoning session model: 700 was spent entirely on thinking, and 4,000 ran out on two of three calls) — through the existing session route (`startLlmBridge`), so no new key, no new endpoint, no new Settings field. The reply is validated and attached only as annotation: `check.advisory`, `critic.counter_metrics`, `critic.generic_controls`. It cannot change a state, and a session with no model route, a failed call, or a non-JSON reply still completes the deterministic pass and records the critic `not_checked` with the reason. `critic: false` makes no model call.
- **New artifact, new summary, no migration.** `extra_checks.json` (`schemaVersion` 1) is written beside the review, so it appears in `meta.artifacts`; `meta.validity` (`run_id`, `round`, `review_run_id`, `input_sha1`, `states`, `flagged_total`, `not_checked_total`, `critic_used`) is a new field on the existing `schemaVersion` 2 store, so nothing needs migrating and a pre-rounds v1 store is still read tolerantly — it is materialized into `rounds/` on this write rather than left half-upgraded, and `meta.params` keeps the review's params. A `validity` row lands in `runs.jsonl`, `status` reports the stored summary and whether it still matches the manuscript, `show section=validity` reads it back, and the pass appends its own notebook entry `deep-review-validity-<run_id>` tagged `validity`.
- **Two corrections to the roadmap's Phase 2 plan**, now recorded in [the roadmap](SCIENTISTTWO-ROADMAP.md). `paper_context` is not used for the local inventory: it takes an external identifier and does network tiering, so it cannot inventory the file under review. And the 4-figures/2-tables versus 9/12 coverage contrast the plan called "the paper's v1 contrast" is in the bundle a **ScientistOne-vs-ScientistTwo** comparison (`sections/4_experiment.tex:13`) whose counts are an author self-report including the appendix.
- **Not wired yet.** `provenance_audit` does not read `extra_checks.json`, so these findings do not appear in a cross-ledger audit; it still joins only the stored `deep_review` claims and citation verdicts. Nothing here gates a turn — only `ah_check` blocks.
- **Phase 0 validity calibration.** `scripts/deep-review-validity-calibration.mjs` (also `deep-review-calibration.mjs --validity --corpus`) runs the four checks over a labeled corpus, converting PDFs with the simple text converter (no OCR; OCR-flagged pages are disclosed, not processed) and reporting state agreement, flag rates, precision/recall, missed clears, abstentions, false alarms, false-positive papers, and determinism. `evaluateValidity` is exported so the harness and the action share one implementation; `scripts/fixtures/deep-review/validity/corpus.json` is the offline self-test, and a local 14-paper measurement corpus (the manifest and JSON report carry absolute paths and stay outside the package; the Markdown report is `reports/deep-review-validity-20261103T235613.md`). On that corpus: 14/14 converted, 0 false-positive papers, 0 false alarms on every check, 43/56 overall agreement, determinism mismatches 0; `attribution` 14/14, `evaluator_drift` 8/14 (one missed clear), `benchmark_coverage` 10/14 and `unmeasured_trade_off` 11/14 (no false alarms; abstentions dominate). The full Markdown report is `reports/deep-review-validity-20261103T235613.md`.
- **Calibrated against real papers.** The corpus loop fixed four general failures: references/bibliography masked before counting (stopping at an appendix/supplementary heading), leading PDF line numbers stripped, coverage counting numeric value columns and treating any second arm as a comparator (with Identity/Grayscale/from-scratch baseline synonyms), and removal evidence requiring an ablation mention to carry a table/figure while a review/synthesis with no mechanism answers `not_checked`. Trade-off vocabularies gained `correct`, `avgbio`, `workload`, `reading time`, and a wider counter window; `HARNESS_PHRASES` dropped bare `shown to`/`displayed`. The first pass had 10 false-positive papers and 39.3% agreement; the final pass has 0 and 76.8%.
- Tests: offline on fixtures modelled on the paper's four cases with `critic: false` — a five-of-six-metrics gain with EMA and label smoothing over a single `Accuracy` column flags `attribution` and `benchmark_coverage`; `act_threshold_frac 0.01 → 0.05` changing what the judge displays flags `evaluator_drift`; a solver-iteration and latency gain reported with speed only flags `unmeasured_trade_off`; a well-formed study with `w/o` arms, baseline and reference rows, three metric columns and three dataset columns, and a latency gain reported beside constraint violation and accuracy flags nothing. Identical bytes reproduce identical states, and a LaTeX comment mentioning latency and ablation is ignored while the monospace threshold change is still caught. `scripts/test-academic-harness.mjs` covers the prompt, skill, and preset surfaces changed here, and `node scripts/build-cordis-patch.mjs --check` the regenerated bundle patch; the committed cases are `scripts/test-deep-review-validity.mjs` (in `npm run test:deep-review`) over `scripts/fixtures/deep-review/validity/`, which adds the unreadable-format `not_checked` path, store-artifact and schema-version checks, and the critic's one-call, annotate-only, failure-tolerant behaviour. The suite also pins the regressions a live Web UI pass found: a commented-out drift, an HTML comment or fenced example table, `"we do not perform an ablation"` as an absence rather than removal evidence, a monospaced evaluator parameter with no harness word, a baseline-free grid beside a cost claim, the v1-store upgrade, the `round`/`run_id` selector, the orphan `rounds/` file a review after `validity` used to write, the stale-PDF conversion gate, and untrusted-tag escaping in evidence. `scripts/test-deep-review-validity-calibration.mjs` runs the corpus harness on the committed fixture corpus and asserts perfect agreement, zero false-positive papers, and zero determinism mismatches. Docs: [Papers](PAPERS.md#evaluation-validity-checks), [Tool index](TOOLS.md), [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md), [Development](DEVELOPMENT.md), [Skills](SKILLS.md), [Documentation home](README.md), [roadmap](SCIENTISTTWO-ROADMAP.md), `README.md`, the `deep-review` skill, `prompt.js`, and the preset's review guidance.

## 0.41.0

Phases 0 and 1 of [ScientistTwo → Academic Harness](SCIENTISTTWO-ROADMAP.md), which documents what the roadmap takes from the paper and what it deliberately does not.

- **`deep_review` keeps review rounds.** Every `review` is now a numbered round: a finding digest in `rounds/<run_id>.json` and its report in `rounds/<run_id>.md`, while `review.json`/`review.md` keep holding the latest pass. Previously the key was derived from the manuscript path and a second review overwrote the only stored report, so a revision's effect could not be measured at all. `meta.json` is schema v2 (`rounds[]`, `latest_round`); a v1 store reads as one round without being rewritten, and the next `review` materializes that pass into `rounds/` first.
- **New `compare` action.** Diffs two rounds — findings closed, opened, and persisting with severity moves, claim-evidence status changes, reproducibility-checklist transitions, and citation verdicts that cleared or newly tripped. Pairing runs by wording, then wording similarity (token overlap ≥ 0.6), then the quoted span; comment ids are tried first, but the engine re-issues them per run, so ids rarely match. `compare` prints a `Matched by` line naming which rule paired the findings, and states whether the manuscript bytes changed: with identical bytes, every difference is reviewer variance. Read-only, makes no model call, and reports readings rather than acceptance.
- **Live end-to-end check.** Driven through the DSH Web UI against the real engine on a scratch manuscript: three rounds on one file, a revision between rounds 1 and 2, a re-review of unchanged bytes, and a hand-written v1 store read and then upgraded. The ladder is non-destructive, the v1 upgrade preserves the original report, and same-round / unknown-round / single-round compare errors surface verbatim. It also caught exact-wording pairing splitting a reworded finding into one closed and one opened — the reason the similarity tier and the `Matched by` line exist — and measured reviewer variance on an unchanged manuscript at 9 findings closed and 6 opened per pass.
- **`status` and `show section=rounds`** expose the ladder with per-round counts, topology, model, and whether each round reviewed the current bytes. Each round records its own model-call and token tally, summed in `status`, so the cost of a review-revise-review loop is visible rather than assumed.
- **Calibration harness.** `scripts/deep-review-calibration.mjs` adds `--collect` (real ratings and decisions through `openreview`; network, no model call) and `--report` (census of stored rounds; reviewer variance as finding overlap between two passes of an unchanged manuscript, and severity agreement across accepted versus rejected papers). Sections report `not_checked` below their sample floor. Not named `test-*.mjs`, because `npm test` globs that prefix and CI would inherit the network dependency. `deep_review` itself needs the host llm bridge, so reviews are produced in a session and measured afterwards.
- **`image_generate` falls back to `mermaid_render` instead of dropping the figure.** When image generation is unconfigured or fails, the agent writes the figure as Mermaid and renders it with Pele, says in chat that it is a Mermaid fallback, and explains how to enable image generation. Real-data plots stay exempt — matplotlib, never a Mermaid stand-in. The tool description, its failure text, and the `visual` schematic template carry the same instruction.
- Tests: `scripts/test-deep-review-tool.mjs` gains a two-round ladder (matching by wording after re-issued ids, citation clearing, checklist and claim transitions), a v1-store upgrade path, and a token-tally assertion; the fixture engine now emits a distinct second-round report. Docs: [Papers](PAPERS.md#deep-review), [Tool index](TOOLS.md), `README.md`, the `deep-review` skill, and the preset's review guidance.

## 0.37.1

- **Remote compute survives transient failures.** Adapted in idea (not code) from `deepseek-ai/dsh-superagent-prototype`: separate "the provider or network could not answer right now" from "your request is wrong", and only retry the first kind.
- `search-http.js` exports `isTransientNetworkError`, `isTransientStatus`, `isTransientError`, `isAuthStatus`, `withTransientRetry`, and `retryQuietly`. Transient means HTTP 408, 429, or 5xx (501 excluded as permanent), or a connection-level failure found by `code` anywhere in the `cause` chain, because undici wraps the real failure in `TypeError: fetch failed`. The HTTP client's fetch-throw path now retries only these, not every thrown error.
- `runpod_run` and `vastai_run` SSH readiness polls tolerate transient blips until their deadline; 401/403 and 404 still fail on the first call.
- `runpod_run`, `vastai_run`, and `modal_run` report `*_transient` (retry the same call), `*_auth` (permanent; fix the credential), or `*_failure`, with the verdict stated in the tool text. `modal_run` also maps gRPC `UNAVAILABLE`/`DEADLINE_EXCEEDED` to transient and `PERMISSION_DENIED`/`UNAUTHENTICATED` to auth. The four hand-rolled teardown loops now share `retryQuietly`; `modal_run` retries idempotent sandbox setup calls but never the user's command.
- Prompt: one bullet of attention discipline (act on what was asked, stay quiet while working, speak at completion, a blocker, or a decision that is the user's).
